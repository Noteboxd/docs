The Noteboxd API gives you programmatic access to one of the most comprehensive fragrance databases available. Whether you're building a recommendation engine, a discovery app, or enriching an e-commerce catalogue, you can query fragrance profiles, olfactory notes, accord families, brand catalogues, perfumer portfolios, and community-powered wearing stats — all over a simple REST API.

## How it works

Noteboxd uses a pay-per-call model. Each request costs between 1¢ and 25¢ and is billed against a prepaid balance tied to your project. There are no subscriptions — you top up your balance and spend only what you use.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a project">
    Sign in to the [Noteboxd developer portal](https://www.noteboxd.com/developers) and create a new project to receive your API key (`nb_live_...`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a prepaid balance">
    Top up your project's balance from the billing section of the portal. Calls are deducted automatically as you make requests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your first request">
    Pass your API key in the `Authorization: Bearer` header and query the API. See the [Quickstart](/quickstart) for a working example.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the data">
    Browse [fragrances](/api-reference/fragrances/list), [notes](/api-reference/notes/list), [brands](/api-reference/brands/get), and [charts](/api-reference/charts/list) to build the experience you have in mind.
  </Step>
</Steps>
